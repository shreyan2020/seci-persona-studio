The `.txt` files preserve the original reports, including references to research artifacts that remain in the separate research workspace. This interface repository bundles the software smoke harness; it does not bundle the complete datasets and training pipelines for every historical experiment.

## Software and interface

- **Local smoke suite:** two tiny retrieval/extraction tasks passed in the [saved run](evaluation-reports/software-smoke.txt). The runnable fixtures and suite configuration are under `backend/evals`. This checks pipeline execution and metric computation.
- **Early retrieval ablation:** ten SciFact queries against 5,183 documents. Query-only and ontology-augmented retrieval both obtained Recall@10 0.7500 and nDCG@10 0.6849; augmentation produced no improvement. [Report](evaluation-reports/scifact-sample10.txt).
- **Application tests:** source binding, board revisions, role orchestration, comparison records, and confirm/revoke behavior have local unit tests. Run the commands in the README for the current checkout; historical benchmark scores are not a substitute for those checks.
- **Itaconic-acid walkthrough:** two papers were inspected and six experimental records corrected against their PDFs. The exported comparisons and role outputs are source-reviewed, edited examples. They demonstrate the workflow, rather than estimate automatic extraction accuracy. [Provenance](example-project.md).

There is no completed researcher study measuring decision quality, effort, trust, or learning, and no controlled evaluation establishes that the three-role interface outperforms a single-agent interface.

## Retrieval and reconciliation research

**Geometric score combination.** On 300 SciFact development claims, 188 had gold evidence and contributed to recall; 112 were excluded from that denominator. Recall@5 was 0.873 for BM25, 0.869 for dense retrieval, 0.906 for reciprocal rank fusion, and 0.862 for Fisher–Rao feedback and its constrained variant. Constrained feedback minus rank fusion was −0.045, with paired interval [−0.088, −0.003]. This experiment did not justify replacing ordinary fusion. [Report](evaluation-reports/geometric-retrieval.txt).

**Context-sensitive passage retrieval.** Equally supervised MiniLM selectors differed in how they sampled negative passages. On 985 additional Evidence Inference questions from 252 papers, excluding the inspected 214-question pilot, three-seed mean Hit@1 was 64.7% with mixed ordinary/context negatives, 61.7% with ordinary negatives, and 58.5% with context-only negatives. The mixed-versus-ordinary difference was +3.0 percentage points, paper-bootstrap interval [+0.7, +5.4]. Intervals are conditional on the three training seeds; the broader test corpus had appeared in earlier work. A hit measures retrieval of an annotated passage, not a correct scientific conclusion. Fisher–Rao/JS acquisition gates did not beat the fixed mixed selector. [Report](evaluation-reports/context-retrieval.txt).

**Fresh-paper reconciliation.** The comparison used 48 questions from 24 Evidence Inference paper IDs not previously used in the local development audit, following adaptation on six exposed cases. Papers came from the dataset's official training partition; inputs were annotation-selected excerpts. With the same local Qwen3.5 reader, direct reading matched 41/48 reference labels, generic review 40/48, and repaired structured reconciliation 36/48. The repaired method was 10.4 percentage points below direct reading, paired interval [−18.8, −2.1]. Missing context and possible reference-label inconsistencies remain unadjudicated. These are reference matches, not independently verified scientific judgments. [Report](evaluation-reports/fresh-paper-reconciliation.txt).

**Role aggregation.** A separate experiment used 40 fresh SciFact cases and 40 fresh PubMedQA training cases with two Qwen readers: 800 generations plus 40 NLI judgments. Direct, generic-review, and repaired-role methods did not show a consistent benefit from the repaired aggregation. The [report](evaluation-reports/role-aggregation.txt) keeps model and dataset results separate. These task-specific role protocols are research experiments, not a user study of the interface's three biotech roles.

## Learned evidence geometry

The proposed geometry tries to preserve both experimental context and fidelity to the source, rather than treating lexical similarity as sufficient. A graph connects findings, and query signals can propagate through those connections using diffusion or wave dynamics. The experiments tested each part instead of assuming that a visually coherent graph was scientifically useful.

**Graph ranking (V9).** Twenty fresh TREC-PM cases contained 480 distinct papers; two zero-ideal cases were retained. Development used eight training and four validation cases already exposed in V8. Mean nDCG@10 was 0.6442 for dense retrieval, 0.7108 for direct 9B relevance, 0.7108 for learned-geometry reranking, 0.7250 for diffusion, and 0.7186 for waves. Wave minus direct was +0.0079 [−0.0155, +0.0305]; wave minus diffusion was −0.0064 [−0.0166, +0.0004]. No wave advantage was established. Clarification answers replayed benchmark relevance grades: **zero actual researcher responses** were collected. Wave-based question selection did not beat random selection in this replay. [Report](evaluation-reports/evidence-graph-v9.txt).

**Context and source fidelity (V10).** Twenty-four previously exposed papers supplied 166 agent-reviewed candidate variants, split by paper into 13 training, three validation, and eight held-out papers. Separate small encoders used frozen Qwen representations. On the held-out papers, learned classification and margin objectives did not beat the pretrained representation for context or faithfulness judgments. Candidate-only faithfulness performance exposed a shortcut; a wrong-denominator example remained closer than the correct one across all three seeds. This did not establish a reliable learned scientific distance. [Report](evaluation-reports/context-geometry-v10.txt).

**Binding measurements to their context (V11).** Twenty-eight exposed abstracts, split 16/4/8 by paper, yielded 1,038 derived pairs. Across 121 controlled records on eight held-out papers, every learned variant recovered zero complete population–measurement owner–count–endpoint tuples, despite nonzero training performance. This was a failed transfer experiment, not evidence that graph propagation solves measurement attribution. [Report](evaluation-reports/binding-geometry-v11.txt).

These results motivate explicit source checks and reversible researcher judgments in the interface. They do not establish a general improvement from geometric learning or multi-agent agreement.
