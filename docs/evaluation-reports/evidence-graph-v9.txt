## What actually learned

The small neural attribute encoder used an eligible training pool of 192 papers (including the internal holdout), with 652 quoted attributes. Its internal weak-relation holdout contains 39 papers; selection epoch 99. Training uses source-bound attribute/context views and sparse cross-paper property/value agreement, not expert causal labels.

Held-out weak-pair loss: learned 0.1534; pretrained-input cosine 0.3899; untrained neural encoder 0.9971. Lower is better for this artificial relation objective; it does not measure scientific causal validity.

Geometry training device: cuda; training wall time: 3.42 seconds. The query projection is learned afterwards from training relevance judgments; the graph does not depend on the current query.

## Verification and scope

Independent rescoring checked 540 rankings, 954 claim anchors and 2470 attribute addresses. Fresh paper-component overlap with prior exposure: 0. Papers with no valid finding: 250.
- train: 304 finding nodes, 1013 typed edges; 70 nodes isolated across all channels.
- val: 152 finding nodes, 445 typed edges; 46 nodes isolated across all channels.
- eval: 748 finding nodes, 2612 typed edges; 194 nodes isolated across all channels.

Quoted text can still be assigned an incorrect role or experimental owner. See SOURCE_REVIEW_DEV.md, the retained failed pilot, and the fresh source review. This model does not discover verified causal DAGs, identify causal effects, or equate propagation strength with scientific support. Its uncertainty is a conditional Gaussian preference surrogate, not calibrated causal confidence. Current PubMed abstracts and public historical judgments do not establish model-pretraining independence.
