# Findings from the second research cycle

The experiments test source selection and grounded comparative reading. They do not establish a novel ML method or solve cross-paper scientific reconciliation.

The useful result is mixed ordinary/context negative sampling: +3.0 percentage points in top-passage annotation hits over ordinary training across three seeds on 985 additional test questions (paper-bootstrap interval +0.7 to +5.4 points). This is an empirical lead, not algorithmic novelty. Pure context sampling and the geometric acquisition gate did not beat the stronger matched baseline.

## The three DSM requirements

| Requirement | Implemented evidence | Remaining boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Collect relevant literature | ELISER terminology expansion with 13 source-backed mdh candidates; modern full-text passage retrieval | No claim of exhaustive literature recall or validated mechanistic expansion |
| Align and reconcile context | Real ICO annotations, three-seed context/mixed learning, source-binding error review | Within-paper evaluation; cross-paper causal reconciliation remains unvalidated |
| State knowns and uncertainties | Source-citing reader diagnostic, explicit unresolved context, source-level ELISER analysis | Citation existence and rationale overlap do not prove complete scientific synthesis |

## Local source-citing reader diagnostic

Qwen3.5:9b (9.7B, Q4_K_M), thinking disabled, 512-token generation cap, 8,192-token context, temperature 0, seed 41 and explicit JSON/label instructions. Eight fixed papers, one question per paper, same reader and prompts across four evidence selectors. This is a diagnostic, not a statistically conclusive answer-quality benchmark or a stronger reasoning-model evaluation.

| Evidence selector | Questions | Accuracy | Accuracy + annotation citation hit | Coverage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| bge | 8 | 75.0% | 62.5% | 87.5% |
| context | 8 | 75.0% | 75.0% | 87.5% |
| mixed | 8 | 87.5% | 75.0% | 100.0% |
| ordinary | 8 | 100.0% | 87.5% | 100.0% |

The remote Qwen3-8B reader was interrupted after a saved response when another GPU workload expanded. A local thinking-enabled attempt exhausted its 3,072-token budget without a final answer. A subsequent format pilot omitted explicit label names in the prompt and produced invalid labels despite supplying a schema; its partial output is preserved separately. The fixed-case diagnostic was restarted with explicit label instructions. None of those partial runs is pooled with the final outputs. Invalid final outputs count as abstentions, not correct no-effect answers.

## Learned acquisition gate and geometry ablation

A CPU-only gate learns which trained selector to use from 40 validation papers (104 questions). Regularization is chosen by four-fold article-grouped cross-validation. Test labels do not enter fitting. This is an exploratory follow-up after inspecting earlier test results, not a new confirmatory holdout.

| Gate features | Hit@1 outside pilot | Difference from fixed mixed |
|---|---:|---:|
| scores_uncertainty | 64.4% | -0.9 points |
| plus_js | 64.6% | -0.7 points |
| plus_fisher_rao | 63.8% | -1.5 points |
| plus_both | 64.6% | -0.7 points |

Neither the learned gate nor Fisher–Rao/JS rank-distribution features beat the fixed mixed selector in this experiment. These score distributions are not calibrated probabilities of scientific truth. Geometry has not earned a place in the contribution.

## What this means for the research question

The meaningful comparison is context-aware learning versus equally supervised ordinary learning. Beating a zero-shot reranker primarily demonstrates the value of domain adaptation. Information gain, gold-answer rewards, ICO conditioning and hard-negative learning already have close precedents; see NOVELTY.md. Full InfoReasoner GRPO was not reproduced.

The unresolved candidate is a joint experimental-context/effect/source acquisition objective, tested against equally rich answer-only supervision. The implemented gate is a single-step selection policy over pretrained selectors; it is not a multi-step policy over joint experimental interpretations. No information-geometric advantage is established.

## Limits and concrete domain result

Gold-rationale overlap is incomplete annotation agreement, not independent entailment. Exact-text deduplication and official article splits do not rule out related trial families or model pretraining contamination. Test expansion includes the pilot; the additional subset is reported separately. The GPU was shared, so wall-clock comparisons are not isolated benchmarks.

ELISER terminology expansion retrieves 13 mdh candidate papers for succinic acid, with source abstracts. Inspection separates computational deletion proposals, enzyme replacement and strain/process interventions. Modification direction is not measured effect direction.
