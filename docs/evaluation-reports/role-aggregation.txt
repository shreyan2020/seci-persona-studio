# Uncertainty and source-role aggregation repair: fresh-case results

The repair fixes a real output-policy defect, but **does not improve on direct reading or generic review in this fresh comparison**. It scores below both controls in all four model/dataset groups. Compared with the old aggregation on the same cases, it gains two SciFact and two PubMedQA matches with Qwen3.5, gains four SciFact matches with Qwen3, and loses four PubMedQA matches with Qwen3. The broader quality hypothesis is not supported.

All 800 fresh generations and 40 NLI classifications completed on the existing A10 server. Eleven mechanism checks passed before fresh inference. No method, reference label, case exclusion or scoring rule changed after fresh inference began. The original V3 experiment remains intact.

## What changed, and what the controls show

The [prospective protocol](PROTOCOL.md) introduces source-linked statements classified as background, aim, method, result, interpretation or unclear. Only exact-scope, same-comparison result/interpretation findings can vote. A valid whole-context NOINFO summary survives without exact findings. A MAYBE summary survives with result/interpretation citations even when those findings are partial. Invalid output remains a separate operational abstention. Source text is returned from the original records rather than regenerated.

Development used 12 exposed V3 cases on both models, with 24 new ledger calls and cached controls. A concrete parser failure on two distinct findings in one paragraph prompted [one pre-fresh amendment](AMENDMENT_01_DUPLICATE_SOURCE.md): allow repeated source IDs, use a set of labels rather than vote counts, and deduplicate returned citations. The original development payload, original scores and deterministic amended replay are retained. No additional development generations were used. [Development findings](DEVELOPMENT.md) already identified polarity and scope failures; acceptance authorized the frozen test, not a quality-success claim.

The fresh ablations isolate an important distinction. Preserving uncertainty in the **old** ledger raises SciFact scores from 27 to 31 and 24 to 31, while leaving PubMedQA at 24 and 23. The full role-aware repair is weaker than that minimal patch on both SciFact comparisons. Relative to its own new raw summaries, aggregation changes scores from 29/31/27/19 to 29/28/26/19 in the table's column order. Thus, the remaining unique-row override causes avoidable losses; added structure does not consistently improve reasoning.

All seven Qwen3.5 and eight Qwen3 NOINFO summaries survive. They are not all correct: Qwen3 preserves one NOINFO that mistakes an opposite result for absence of evidence. For MAYBE, Qwen3.5 retains 12/13 summaries and Qwen3 retains 19/22. Two losses arise because observed results are omitted or classified as methods, leaving no result citation; two more arise from invalid final source addresses. The end-to-end system therefore still loses some uncertainty answers even though the deterministic preservation rule passes its checks.

Correct NOINFO recall is 7/14 for both repaired models, versus 3/14 and 0/14 for old aggregation. Direct/generic achieve 9/14 and 10/14 with Qwen3.5, and 7/14 and 6/14 with Qwen3. Correct MAYBE recall rises from 2/6 to 5/6 for Qwen3.5 and remains 5/6 for Qwen3. Better uncertainty recall can coexist with worse total accuracy and more wrong affirmative/negative answers. [Mechanism diagnostics](fresh_001/evaluation/mechanism_diagnostics.json) retain the separate error categories.

## Source review and citation limits

All 80 supplied contexts received query/role/scope review notes **before generative predictions were opened**. Some SciFact references had already been seen in a separate review of the 16 NLI errors; this was not a fully label-blinded annotation exercise. Subsequently, all 80 Qwen3.5 ledgers (162 findings) were inspected, along with every case selected by candidate/direct/generic correctness changes or old/full label changes: 29 primary and 23 secondary records, with 12 shared case IDs. This is assistant review, not independent expert adjudication. No source concern changed a benchmark label or denominator.

- **A faithful repair:** `pubmedqa_17489316` asks about both VEGF/venous invasion and survival. Qwen3.5 separates the study aim from the reported survival result and retains MAYBE. Controls had inferred the invasion link from the aim or group definitions.
- **Uncertainty recovered:** `scifact_655` lacks the specific infusion/route/mRNA finding. Both models retain NOINFO where old aggregation abstained. Similar recoveries concern missing prostate/autophagy, strand-direction and numerical-threshold evidence.
- **A correct summary overwritten:** for `pubmedqa_17323047`, Qwen3.5 correctly summarizes that off-hours treatment was not delayed, but a YES-labeled result row changes the final answer to YES. Qwen3 loses three correct SciFact raw summaries through the same polarity-override mechanism (`853`, `1205`, `856`).
- **Setup still misclassified:** Qwen3 labels a no-reperfusion protocol description as a result in `pubmedqa_10757151`; actual infarct outcomes occur elsewhere. In `pubmedqa_18096128`, it excludes the hypothesis correctly but falsely claims results are absent, ignoring the supplied neck-tenderness findings.
- **A reference gain that is not faithful science:** in `scifact_169`, both repaired models treat an all-cancer null as a postmenopausal breast-cancer null. This gains a reference match over direct NOINFO while overreaching the supplied endpoint/population.
- **Other failures survive correct roles:** missing anatomical qualifiers, prevention versus treatment, crude versus adjusted associations, corrected p values, and dementia versus subjective memory impairment. Rejecting all background also loses useful contextual assertions; roles and entailment are separate questions.

Every returned citation resolves to the exact original source record, but generation still makes address errors. Across 800 raw outputs, rationale-first produces five invalid final source addresses and one malformed JSON response; generic review produces one malformed response; the new ledger produces two invalid final addresses. All are retained as failures. The two new-ledger errors use `[2,3]` when both findings came from source `2` and no source `3` exists, consistent with confusing finding positions and source IDs. Repeated source IDs occur in 4/80 and 9/80 new ledgers and are handled by the pre-fresh amendment. Soft length/count deviations are reported, not retrospectively excluded. [Raw-output audit](fresh_001/evaluation/output_structure_audit.json).

SciFact sentence F1 rises from direct .673 to repaired .729 for Qwen3.5, and .609 to .652 for Qwen3. Yet correct-label plus complete-rationale counts fall from 23/26 to 21/26 and from 20/26 to 19/26. These supplied-abstract diagnostics are not official end-to-end SciFact scores. PubMedQA has no gold spans here. Result-only citations can also omit methods needed to identify treatment arms, although those methods remain in the ledger. Source-address validity and span overlap do not establish scientific support.

## Execution and data boundaries

The cohort contains 40 previously unused local-comparison SciFact training cases and 40 PubMedQA human-labeled fold-0 training questions, one paper ID per dataset case. Selection was deterministic and label-agnostic after development acceptance. SciFact references are 18 SUPPORT, 8 CONTRADICT and 14 NOINFO; PubMedQA has 21 YES, 13 NO and 6 MAYBE. V3 question/paper IDs, known prior training-pilot papers and exposed exact normalized contexts were excluded. PubMedQA conclusions were withheld. No official test/validation or sealed/reserved cohort was opened. Reference labels stayed local. Historical SciFact corpus exposure and pretraining contamination remain unresolved; local-ID freshness does not establish a fresh corpus or complete cross-dataset paper independence.

The same cached BF16 Qwen3.5-9B and Qwen3-8B revisions, greedy decoding, seed 41, disabled thinking and 8192/850 input/output token ceilings were used. All nine weight shards match V3. All four original generation-control prompts are unchanged; all 800 actual prompts match the frozen scorer's reconstruction. The added role prompt changes the requested representation and instructions together, so its effect is not a pure isolated source-role intervention. Same token ceilings do not imply equal actual compute.

Fresh generation used **5,075.3 request seconds**: 2,581.1 for Qwen3.5 and 2,494.2 for Qwen3. NLI used 12.3 CPU request seconds. The complete fresh job ran from 10:40:20.520782 to 12:05:46 UTC on 9 November 2026, **85.4 minutes**, within the two-hour limit. Development separately used 24 calls and 219.0 request seconds, with substantial cold-loading overhead. No inference exception or truncation occurred. No new machine, fine-tuning, generative-weight download or paid API was used. Cache warming during the development weight audit prevents treating these times as a controlled speed comparison. The server GPU was verified idle after completion.

The archive SHA256 matches, 701 existing local artifacts matched byte-for-byte, and no existing file was overwritten during final import. Independent checks recomputed accuracy, macro-F1, confusion counts and paired wins/losses; verified original citation objects, freshness, target/method/weight hashes; and matched the primary interim analysis and both overlapping secondary snapshots to final predictions. Snapshot counts are repeated integrity checks, not extra cases. [Import receipt](fresh_001/import_receipt.json), [independent verification](fresh_001/evaluation/independent_verification.json), [frozen manifest](freeze.json), [selection audit](fresh_001/selection_audit.json), [execution record](experiment_record.json).

Retain uncertainty preservation as a necessary policy correction. Do not adopt the full repaired aggregation as an accuracy improvement over direct or generic review. Before another fresh test, address silent row-label overrides, missing comparator/qualifier binding and source-role/citation generation errors on exposed development data, with independent semantic review. Keep the minimal uncertainty patch and raw-summary controls. No wave propagation, general scientific-reasoning advance or novelty claim is established by this experiment.
